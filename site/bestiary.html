Bestiary

The keepers of the Library

Ten creatures, each with its own model and three or four variants that fight differently. The Restricted Section rolls the harder ones.

Lumen Moth · Silent Librarian · Ink Golem · Vault Ward · Inkling · Page Wraith · Tome Mimic · Bookwyrm · Quill Stalker · Bookend Gargoyle · The Archivist

Lumen Moth

Harmless light4 variants

A drifting mote of lamp-light. It will not hurt you; the variants are the point.

  • Common: sheds Lumen Dust
  • Ember: lights candles
  • Pale: gives Night Vision
  • Void: brings Darkness

Silent Librarian

Neutral until you steal4 variants

Shh. Neutral until you take a tome from a chest or shelf nearby, then it never forgets.

  • Shusher: silences you with Slowness and Weakness
  • Archivist's Clerk: steals a stack and runs
  • Censor: blinds you and vanishes
  • Head Librarian: rallies the other keepers

Ink Golem

Tank3 variants

Slow, heavy and stained. Found in scriptoriums.

  • Ink: leaves slowing pools
  • Iron-bound: winds up a slam that launches you
  • Blotted: bursts into Inklings
The Iron-bound's slam is telegraphed: watch the wind-up and step away.

Vault Ward

Stationary turret4 variants

It does not chase you. It guards.

  • Sentinel: shields allies
  • Bulwark: reflects projectiles
  • Seeker: fires homing bolts
  • Warden of Keys: seals a vault's chests until it falls
A Bulwark reflects projectiles, so fight it up close.

Inkling

Small and many4 variants

Ink given a grudge.

  • Black: splits in two
  • Blue: slows you
  • Red: swells and bursts
  • Gilded: is shy and drops dust
Back away from a swelling Red Inkling before it bursts.

Page Wraith

Dives from above3 variants

A sheet of loose pages that circles and dives.

  • Gilded: shields its allies
  • Cursed: weakens you
  • Blank: copies the damage of your weapon

Tome Mimic

Sleeps as a book3 variants

Looks like a book on the floor until you come close.

  • Pocket: bites and runs
  • Grimoire: casts hexes
  • Ledger: swallows your items

Bookwyrm

Burrows through shelves2 variants

It tunnels through shelving and bursts out of another; it rattles first.

  • Glossy: shrugs off hits from the front
  • Ancient: eats books to heal
Listen for the rattle, and hit a Glossy Bookwyrm from the side.

Quill Stalker

Climbs walls3 variants

Eight legs, one long quill.

  • Needle: fires quills
  • Inkfang: leaps and poisons
  • Scribe: binds you with a rune

Bookend Gargoyle

A statue until it notices you3 variants

Perched on the ends of shelves, perfectly still.

  • Granite: moves only when unwatched
  • Marble: pounces
  • Obsidian: deflects arrows
Keep your eyes on a Granite Gargoyle; it only moves when you look away.

The Archivist

Boss360 health3 phases

The Library's guardian waits in the sanctums of the Restricted Section. Every attack is telegraphed and counterable.

  • Page Storm: aim lines lock before the volley. Sidestep.
  • Erasure: marked floor bands are wiped by a wave that deletes placed blocks and your longest-lasting buff. Step off.
  • Recall and Page Slam: a tether drags you to the dais. Break line of sight behind a pillar to snap it and stagger the Archivist, or run out of the ring.
  • The Index: summoned Vault Wards shield it until they're destroyed.

Drops: Mastered tomes, exclusive tomes, a pile of Lumen Dust, the Archivist's Seal and the Archivist's Bookmark (sneak-use to mark a spot, hold use to return there; 60-second cooldown, same dimension).
